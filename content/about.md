I'm Diego Montúfar Pérez, a **Technical Artist** who bridges the gap between art and code. My work focuses on creating tools, shaders, and pipelines that empower artists while maintaining technical excellence.

## My Philosophy

As a Technical Artist, I believe in:

- **Empowering Creativity:** Building tools that let artists focus on what they do best—creating amazing visuals
- **Efficient Workflows:** Automating repetitive tasks and streamlining pipelines to save time and reduce errors
- **Problem-Solving:** Finding elegant technical solutions to complex artistic challenges
- **Continuous Learning:** Staying current with emerging technologies and techniques in game development and visual effects

## What I Do

I specialize in the intersection of art and technology, with expertise in:

### Tools & Pipeline Development
Creating Python-based tools and scripts that automate workflows in Maya, Blender, and other DCC applications. From asset preparation to quality control, I build solutions that make production pipelines more efficient.

### Shader Development
Designing custom shaders and materials using HLSL and node-based systems. Whether it's stylized rendering, PBR workflows, or performance optimization, I create visual solutions that look great and run efficiently.

### Technical Implementation
Implementing art features in Unity and Unreal Engine, including VFX systems, procedural content generation, and asset optimization for various platforms.

## Background

With a Bachelor's degree in Digital Art and Animation from Tecnológico de Monterrey and currently pursuing dual Master's degrees in Technical Art and 3D Modelling at COCO School, I combine formal training with hands-on industry experience.

I've worked on shipped titles including **Luma Island** and **Giggleland**, where I've developed modular environment systems, created optimization tools, and implemented art pipelines from concept to production.

## Let's Connect

I'm always interested in discussing technical art challenges, sharing knowledge, and collaborating on interesting projects.
